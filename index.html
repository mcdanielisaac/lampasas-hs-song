School Songs

Alma Mater

Lampasas High

Lampasas High to you we're proudly singing.
Our voices raise to you in highest praise.
We love each stone, your ivy covered entrance,
Where all our youth, we struggle to succeed.

The sportsmanship, the loyalty, the teachers,
The colors of our tried old white and blue,
And through the years we'll labor to retain them,
And to our alma mater we will be true.

To the tune of the Irish folk song "Londonderry Air"
"Lampasas High" lyrics (c. 1939–1940) by Herbert Abney Jr., Lampasas High School class of 1940.

Fight Song

I'm a Badger

Cause I'm a Badger, Badger, Badger up in Lampus in the hills.
Cause I'm a Badger, Badger, Badger
With a sisboom hip hurrah, rah, rah, rah.
Got the claws sharp enough to rip the bulldog's tail,
Pull their beards and listen to the hillbillies wail.
Cause I'm a Badger, Badger, Badger riding on a Texas gale.

Go. Fight. Badgers fight.
Go. Fight. Win tonight.

To the tune of "I'm a Jayhawk" (University of Kansas fight song).
Written and composed by George H. "Dumpy" Bowles, 1912.
"I'm a Badger" lyrics (c. 1955–1956) by Velma Harrison, Lampasas High School history teacher and girls' basketball coach.
